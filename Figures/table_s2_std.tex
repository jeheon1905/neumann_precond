\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{%
Per-iteration cost of the Damped-NP (order $=5$) and ISI preconditioners
under \emph{fixed-Hamiltonian} diagonalization \emph{without the locking
scheme}, summarized over the steady-state region (the first and last
iterations are excluded because their preconditioning times are undefined:
the first iteration has no preconditioning step and the last iteration is a
partial/convergence step). For each benchmark system, the mean and sample
standard deviation ($\mu \pm \sigma$, in seconds) of the diagonalization time
and the preconditioning time are listed for both methods. The third row of
each block reports the standard-deviation ratio
$\sigma_{\mathrm{ISI}}/\sigma_{\text{Damped-NP}}$ for the corresponding
quantity. Across all four systems Damped-NP maintains an essentially constant
per-iteration cost ($\sigma \lesssim 0.006$~s for both quantities), whereas
the variability of ISI is larger by a factor of roughly $5$--$106$; the
largest disparity occurs for $\mathrm{MAPbI}_{3}$ (preconditioning time,
$\sim\!106\times$) and the smallest for Vitamin~$\mathrm{B}_{12}$
($\sim\!5\times$).%
}
\label{tab:s2_per_iter_std}
